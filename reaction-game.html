⚡ About the Reaction Speed Test

Reaction Speed Test is a simple browser-based challenge that measures how quickly you respond to a visual signal. The goal is to wait for the correct signal and react as quickly as possible.

Each attempt gives you a reaction-time result measured in milliseconds. You can repeat the test and try to improve your previous result.

🎮 How to Play

  1. Press the Start Test button.
  2. Wait for the reaction area to change.
  3. Click the reaction area as quickly as you can.
  4. Check your reaction-time result.
  5. Try again and compare your new result with your previous attempt.

📊 Understanding Your Result

Your result is displayed in milliseconds (ms). A lower number means that the recorded response took less time during that particular attempt.

Reaction results can vary from one attempt to another because of factors such as timing, device performance, browser performance, screen interaction, and the conditions in which you play.

💡 Reaction Test Tips

  • Read the instructions before starting.
  • Make sure you can clearly see the reaction area.
  • Use a comfortable screen and playing position.
  • Focus on the visual signal instead of guessing when it will appear.
  • Try several attempts instead of judging your performance from one result.

❓ Frequently Asked Questions

What does this game measure?

It records the time between the game's visual signal and your interaction with the reaction area.

Is the test free?

Yes. You can play the Reaction Speed Test directly in your browser.

Can I play on a phone?

Yes. The game is designed for browser-based play on supported mobile devices and computers.

Why are my results different each time?

Reaction results naturally vary between attempts. Device, browser, screen interaction, timing, and playing conditions can all affect the recorded result.

Can I try the test multiple times?

Yes. Repeating the challenge lets you compare your results across different attempts.
