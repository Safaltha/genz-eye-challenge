🎯 Game Rules

Each round gives you a target animal. Multiple animals appear together, so you must quickly identify the correct one.

You need 10 correct hits to complete a round. The target appears many times, so you have enough opportunities to complete the round.

Correct hits increase your remaining time. Wrong hits reduce it. Missing targets also costs time.

Bombs end the game immediately. Special animals give bonus points and extra time.
