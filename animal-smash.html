🎯 Game Rules

Each round gives you a target animal. Multiple animals appear together, so you must quickly identify the correct one.

You need 10 correct hits to complete a round. The target appears many times, so you have enough opportunities to complete the round.

Correct hits increase your remaining time. Wrong hits reduce it. Missing targets also costs time.

Bombs end the game immediately. Special animals give bonus points and extra time.

🐾 About Animal Smash

Animal Smash is a fast-paced browser game that combines visual attention, target recognition, and quick decision-making. Each round presents several animals on the game board while one animal is selected as the target.

Your goal is to identify the correct target and click it before the opportunity disappears. As the difficulty increases, the game becomes faster and introduces more distractions.

The game also includes combos, bonus animals, lives, time management, and different difficulty settings to make each session more challenging.

🎮 Difficulty Guide

🟢 Easy

A slower mode designed for learning the game mechanics and becoming familiar with the target animals.

🟡 Normal

A balanced mode with faster targets and more distractions. This is a good choice for regular gameplay.

🔴 Hard

The fastest mode with greater pressure and less time to identify the correct target.

🏆 Scoring and Progress

Your score increases when you successfully hit the target animal. Consecutive correct hits can build a combo and improve your reward.

Special animals can provide additional points or time. Your best score is displayed so you can compare future sessions with your previous performance.

Results can vary between sessions because the game uses changing target positions, timing, difficulty settings, and random events.

❓ Frequently Asked Questions

What is the goal of Animal Smash?

Find and click the animal shown as the current target while avoiding incorrect targets and hazards.

How do I start the game?

Choose a difficulty and press the Start Game button. Follow the target shown at the top of the game board.

What are bonus animals?

Bonus animals are special targets that can provide additional rewards according to the game's current rules.

Why does the game become harder?

Higher difficulty settings increase the speed and challenge of identifying the correct target.

Can I play on a phone?

Yes. Animal Smash is designed as a browser game and can be played on supported mobile devices and computers.

Is Animal Smash free?

Yes. The game is available to play directly through the website without requiring a separate application.
