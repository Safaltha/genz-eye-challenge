🎯 Round Rules

Every round gives you a target animal and a limited amount of time. Hit the target enough times to automatically advance to the next round.

Other animals are distractions. The special animal gives bonus points. Bombs are dangerous and must never be hit.

The game becomes gradually faster and harder. The target requirement can increase, but it will never be more than 10 hits per round.

If a difficult round becomes too hard, you can return to the difficulty menu and start again on Easy, Normal, or Hard.
