🎮 About Gen Z Tic Tac Toe

Gen Z Tic Tac Toe is a browser-based strategy game based on the classic three-by-three Tic Tac Toe board. You can play against another person or challenge the computer at different difficulty levels.

The objective is simple: place three of your symbols in a horizontal, vertical, or diagonal line before your opponent does. Although the rules are easy to learn, making the right move becomes more challenging when playing against a stronger computer opponent.

Each match is independent, allowing you to practice different strategies and improve your decision-making over multiple games.

🕹️ How to Play Tic Tac Toe

  1. Choose Play With Friend or Play With Computer.
  2. If you choose the computer, select a difficulty level.
  3. Click an empty square to place your symbol.
  4. Try to create a line of three matching symbols.
  5. At the same time, watch your opponent's possible winning moves and block them when necessary.
  6. The game ends when one player wins or all nine squares are occupied.

📋 Tic Tac Toe Rules

  • ❌ X and ⭕ O take turns placing symbols.
  • 🎯 A player wins by creating three matching symbols in a row.
  • ↔️ A winning line can be horizontal, vertical, or diagonal.
  • 🤝 If every square is filled without a winning line, the match is a draw.
  • 🔄 Starting a new match clears the current board.

🤖 Computer Difficulty Levels

😊 Easy

A beginner-friendly level that gives you an opportunity to learn the board and practice basic moves.

😎 Medium

A more challenging opponent that requires greater attention to both your own moves and possible threats.

🔥 Hard

A demanding level for players who want to test their planning and defensive decision-making.

🧠 Tic Tac Toe Strategy Tips

Winning Tic Tac Toe is not only about trying to create your own line. You also need to pay attention to the moves available to your opponent.

  1. Look for a winning move: If you can complete a line, take the opportunity.
  2. Block threats: If your opponent is one move away from winning, consider blocking that position.
  3. Create multiple possibilities: Try to make moves that give you more than one possible winning line.
  4. Think ahead: Before placing a symbol, consider what your opponent could do next.

🏆 Understanding the Scoreboard

The scoreboard records the results of your matches. The X and O counters show how many games each side has won, while the Draw counter records matches where neither side completes a winning line.

❌ X Score

Number of matches won by X.

🤝 Draw

Number of matches ending without a winner.

⭕ O Score

Number of matches won by O.

👥 Playing With a Friend

Friend mode allows two people to play on the same device. Players take turns selecting empty squares and can use the mode to practice different strategies against another person.

Because both players can see the complete board, the challenge comes from predicting the other player's next move and finding opportunities to create a winning line.

❓ Frequently Asked Questions

How do I start a game?

Choose a game mode first. If you select computer mode, choose a difficulty level before making your moves.

How does a player win?

A player wins by placing three of their symbols in a horizontal, vertical, or diagonal line.

What happens when the board is full?

If all nine squares are occupied and neither player has created a winning line, the match ends in a draw.

Can two people play together?

Yes. Select Play With Friend to play locally on the same device.

What are the computer difficulty levels?

Computer mode provides Easy, Medium, and Hard options. Each level is intended to provide a different challenge.

Can I reset the scoreboard?

Yes. Use the Reset Score button to clear the recorded scoreboard.
