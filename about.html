👁️ About Gen Z Eye Challenge

Gen Z Eye Challenge is an interactive vision and observation game designed to test your focus, attention, and problem-solving skills.

🎮 Our Mission

Our goal is to create fun challenges that improve observation ability while giving users an exciting gaming experience.

🔥 Features

  • 👁️ Eye observation challenges
  • 🧠 Text quiz games
  • 🤖 AI Eye Coach hints
  • 🏆 Score and level system
  • ⚡ Future daily challenges

🌟 Why Play?

Challenge yourself, improve your focus, and compete with your friends.
