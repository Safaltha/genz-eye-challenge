🦅 About Eagle Radar

Eagle Radar is a visual memory challenge where you need to remember the location of a target before it disappears. After the target is hidden, your task is to identify or select the location you remember.

The challenge becomes more difficult as you progress through the levels. Pay attention to the target's position before the game moves to the next stage.

🎮 How to Play Eagle Radar

  1. Press the START button to begin.
  2. Watch the target carefully while it is visible.
  3. Remember its location on the game area.
  4. Follow the instruction shown after the target disappears.
  5. Continue through the levels and try to improve your score.

🎯 Game Objective

Your objective is to correctly remember the target location and complete as many successful rounds as possible. Your score, level, lives, coins, and combo can change as you play.

📈 Difficulty and Progress

Eagle Radar is designed to become more challenging as you progress. Higher levels may require greater attention and more accurate memory. Your progress is shown through the game's level and progress indicators.

💡 Tips for Playing

  • 👀 Watch the target carefully before it disappears.
  • 🧠 Try to remember its position relative to the game area.
  • 🎯 Avoid clicking randomly when you are unsure.
  • ⏱️ Take your time to understand the instruction before responding.
  • 🔁 Replay the game if you want to improve your previous result.

❓ Eagle Radar FAQ

Is Eagle Radar free to play?

Yes. Eagle Radar is available to play directly in your web browser.

Do I need to install an app?

No. You can open the game in a supported web browser.

Can I play Eagle Radar on a phone?

Yes. The game is designed to work on compatible mobile devices and desktop browsers.

What does the score mean?

The score is a gameplay measure used to track your performance within the challenge. It is intended for entertainment and personal comparison rather than as a professional measurement of memory or visual ability.
